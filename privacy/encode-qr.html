Privacy Policy — encode QR

Developer: zeroZenDevLab
Last updated: 2026-05-18

Overview

encode QR is a QR code generator for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or login required.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No advertising SDKs or tracking.
  • Text and URLs entered by the user are used solely to generate the QR code and are never sent to any server.
  • Images selected from the device are used locally to compose the QR code and are never uploaded.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. QR images are exported to the device gallery using the Android MediaStore API, which does not require storage permissions on Android 10 (API 29) and above.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party service, SDK, or API.

Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children.
