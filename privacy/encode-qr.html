Privacy Policy — encode QR

Developer: zeroZenDevLab
Data Controller: zeroZenDevLab (Italy)
Last updated: 2026-06-06

Overview

encode QR is a QR code generator for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or login required.
  • No analytics or crash reporting.
  • No advertising SDKs or tracking.
  • Text and URLs entered by the user are used solely to generate the QR code and are never sent to any server.
  • Images selected from the device are used locally to compose the QR code and are never uploaded.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. QR images are exported to the device gallery using the Android MediaStore API, which does not require storage permissions on Android 10 (API 29) and above.

Distribution channel

The app is distributed via Google Play Store. Install events, crash reports, ratings, and reviews are collected by Google under Google's own privacy policy, independently of this app. We have no access to or control over Google's data collection. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party service, SDK, or API at runtime.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict the processing of, port, and object to the processing of any personal data we hold about you. Since the app collects no personal data, there is nothing on our end to access, rectify, or delete. To exercise these rights or for clarification, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app does not collect data from any user, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR Article 8). The app is suitable for all ages.
