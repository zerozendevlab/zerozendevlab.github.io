Last updated: 2026-06-06

Overview

link-tracker is a self-hosted web service that turns a single shared URL (/go/<slug>) into a tracked redirect to a destination of the operator's choice. The service is designed to log enough to count clicks per channel, and nothing more. This policy applies to the live instance at the URL above.

Data we do NOT collect

When you click a /go/<slug> link served by this instance, we do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your IP address.
  • Your browser or User-Agent string.
  • Cookies on the public redirect endpoint.
  • Any device fingerprint or hardware identifier.
  • Your geographic location.
  • The Referer header.
  • Any other information that could identify you, directly or indirectly.

This is enforced at the application level: the data is never even read into memory.

Data we DO collect

For each click on a /go/<slug> URL, the service stores a single record containing:

  • A UTC timestamp of the click.
  • The slug that was clicked (e.g. yt-encodeqr).
  • The internal source and target node identifiers associated with the slug.

None of these fields qualify as personal data under GDPR Article 4(1), because they cannot be associated with an identifiable natural person.

Cookies

The public /go/<slug> endpoint sets no cookies.

The administration area (/admin/*) sets one strictly necessary session cookie when an authorized administrator logs in. This cookie is used only to maintain the authenticated session and is marked Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite=Lax. Under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, strictly necessary cookies do not require user consent.

Hosting and data location

The service is hosted on PythonAnywhere, a UK-based provider. Following the UK GDPR adequacy decision (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1772), personal data transfers between the EU and the UK take place without additional safeguards. Click records are stored in a SQLite database file on the hosting server. No data is transferred outside the UK or to any third party.

Third-party services

The service does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tag manager, CDN, or external SDK at runtime. No data is shared with any third party.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict the processing of, port, and object to the processing of any personal data the service holds about you. Since the public click traffic produces no personal data by design, there is nothing on our end to access, rectify, delete, or port. For administrative accounts (operators), you may request access or deletion of your authentication record by contacting us at the address below.

Retention

Click records (timestamp + slug + internal ids) are retained indefinitely for the operator's own usage statistics. Because no personal data is stored, retention does not create a GDPR data subject impact. The operator may delete records at any time from the administration area.

Children

The service does not knowingly collect data from any user, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR Article 8). Because no personal data is collected from public click traffic, the service is compatible with use by minors.
